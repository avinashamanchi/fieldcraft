FieldCraft Support

For help, bug reports, privacy requests, or account-deletion problems, open an issue in the FieldCraft GitHub repository.

Do not include passwords, access tokens, customer contact details, invoice contents, receipt images, or other private business data in a public issue.

Manual invoice and expense entry remains available when optional AI or native recognition is unavailable.

Subscriptions

Use Restore purchases in FieldCraft when an eligible subscription is missing. Manage or cancel it in Apple subscription settings. For an App Store charge or refund request, use Apple purchase support. Apple decides refund eligibility under its policies.

Deleting a FieldCraft account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Do not post receipts, Apple account details, or payment information in a public issue.

Customer invoice payments

Customer invoice payments are processed on Stripe's hosted checkout for a connected trade business; they do not purchase FieldCraft Pro. A return to FieldCraft can remain pending until a signed Stripe webhook confirms the result. For a refund, disputed charge, service issue, or receipt for a real-world job, contact the business named on the invoice first. Do not send full card or bank details to FieldCraft support or place them in a public issue.

Payment reminders

You can disable a reminder schedule from its invoice. If a reminder is marked pending, delivered, or bounced incorrectly, report only the content-free delivery identifier and approximate time—never customer email, invoice text, or access tokens—in a public issue.
