FieldCraft Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026.

FieldCraft is a business-management tool for jobs, clients, invoices, expenses, services, inventory, and business settings. It is not accounting, tax, legal, or financial advice. You are responsible for reviewing every invoice and business record.

Storage

FieldCraft keeps an offline cache on your device so you can work without a network. Authenticated records are synchronized to Supabase cloud services. A business logo you explicitly select is rasterized and stored in an owner-only Supabase Storage path. Cloud records remain until you use Delete Account; the offline cache remains until you delete local data or remove the app.

Optional AI processing

AI features are off until you consent. An invoice transcript you submit, or reviewed expense vendor, amount, and notes, may be sent through FieldCraft's authenticated server function to Groq for processing. The provider output can be inaccurate and must be reviewed. FieldCraft does not upload raw audio or a raw receipt image to Groq. Speech recognition and receipt OCR run on the device in a FieldCraft development or App Store build.

Data not collected for advertising

FieldCraft uses no tracking, behavioral advertising, third-party ad SDK, contact-book access, payment access, or automatic customer messaging. It does not claim that opening a share sheet delivered an invoice. Limited content-free security metadata such as request identifiers, route names, rate-limit digests, status, and latency buckets may be retained to operate the AI boundary.

Your controls

You can revoke AI consent, clear the local offline cache, or use Delete Account to permanently remove your Supabase account, synchronized records, and owner-scoped logo. If cleanup is interrupted, FieldCraft reports the incomplete areas and offers a retry.

Support

See the FieldCraft Support page for help or privacy requests.
