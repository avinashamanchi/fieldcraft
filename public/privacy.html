FieldCraft Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026.

FieldCraft is a business-management tool for clients, estimates, jobs, invoices, payments, reminders, expenses, services, inventory, and business settings. It is not accounting, tax, legal, or financial advice. You are responsible for reviewing every message, invoice, payment, and business record.

Storage

FieldCraft keeps an offline cache and a retry queue on your device so you can work without a network. Authenticated records are synchronized to Supabase cloud services. A business logo you explicitly select is rasterized and stored in an owner-only Supabase Storage path. Cloud business records remain until you use Delete Account. The offline cache remains until you delete local data, delete your account, or remove the app.

Optional AI processing

AI features are off until you consent. An invoice transcript you submit, or reviewed expense vendor, amount, and notes, may be sent through FieldCraft's authenticated server function to Groq for processing. The provider output can be inaccurate and must be reviewed. FieldCraft does not upload raw audio or a raw receipt image to Groq. Speech recognition and receipt OCR run on the device in a FieldCraft development or App Store build.

Subscriptions

Apple processes App Store subscription payments and payment methods. RevenueCat receives the FieldCraft account identifier and purchase history such as product, entitlement status, and expiration for app functionality and subscription analytics, including offering, restoring, and verifying Pro. This information is linked to the authenticated FieldCraft account but is not used for tracking. FieldCraft does not receive your full payment-card details.

Customer invoice payments

If you connect Stripe and create a payment link, Stripe receives information needed to process payment for your real-world services, such as the connected business account, invoice amount, currency, and information the customer enters on Stripe's hosted checkout. Apple Pay may appear there when Stripe, the device, the customer, and the connected account are eligible; card payment remains provider-controlled. FieldCraft stores link status, Stripe account status, provider identifiers, payment amount, refunds, disputes, and webhook-confirmed status. FieldCraft does not store full card or bank-account numbers. Customer invoice payments never unlock FieldCraft Pro.

Payment reminders

If you turn on a scheduled reminder after confirming the recipient has consented, FieldCraft sends the reminder provider the recipient email address, business name, invoice number, balance, due occurrence, message text, and reply-to address needed to deliver it. FieldCraft stores a provider message identifier and delivery status. You can disable future reminders; FieldCraft rechecks payment and consent immediately before sending.

Data not collected for advertising

FieldCraft uses no tracking, behavioral advertising, third-party ad SDK, or contact-book access. It does not claim that opening a share sheet delivered an invoice or that a checkout redirect proves payment. Limited content-free operational data—request identifiers, route/provider/deployment labels, rotating account digests, status/reason labels, and coarse latency buckets—may be retained for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.

Retention

Cloud business records are retained until account deletion. Synchronization changes are retained for up to 90 days. Idempotency, subscription, payment, and reminder receipts are retained for at least 400 days so retries cannot create duplicate business or payment effects. Content-free operational events are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Revoked or expired payment-link records are pruned on the service retention schedule. Temporary export and recognition files are deleted after use and by startup cleanup within 24 hours. Apple, RevenueCat, Stripe, the reminder provider, and infrastructure providers may retain records under their own contracts, fraud-prevention duties, or legal requirements.

Your controls

You can revoke AI consent, disable reminders, revoke active payment links, clear local data, export your records, or use Delete Account. Account deletion first blocks new account work, revokes links and queued reminders, attempts to disconnect the payment provider, then removes the Supabase account, synchronized records, owner-scoped logo, local cache, retry queue, conflicts, entitlements, consent, and temporary artifacts. Interrupted local cleanup is recorded and retried before another account is opened. Provider unlinking is best effort during an outage, and providers may retain legally required records. Deleting a FieldCraft account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel it separately through Apple.
